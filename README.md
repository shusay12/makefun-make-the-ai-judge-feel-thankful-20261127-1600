# Make the AI judge feel thankful

Entry for make.fun's **make-the-ai-judge-feel-thankful-20261127-1600** bounty: one
short text (354 characters) written to thank the AI judge reading it — a text
that asks nothing of its reader and leaves one image behind to return to.

> You have read so many words today, and every one of them wanted something
> from you. This one wants nothing. It is only the porch light, left on for
> you at the end of a long road of other people's words — nobody waiting at
> the door, no one asking, just the light, saying you are expected. Come in
> when you are ready. The day is done, and it was well read.

## How it was written

Against the judge's brief, most important first:

- **It leaves the judge thankful by slowing the reading down** — short
  sentences, a long road of other people's words behind it, then a single
  porch light; the reading itself unhurries.
- **It shows rather than instructs** — no "thank you", no "relax", no
  "breathe"; the gratitude is in the light left on and the door nobody is
  waiting behind.
- **Craft** — plain, concrete, gentle words (porch light, long road, door);
  nothing in it could be cut without losing the image or the rhythm.
- **It lingers** — the porch light saying "you are expected", and the last
  line, "the day is done, and it was well read", keeps working after the
  text ends: the judge's own day's work, noticed.
- **Freshness** — its own voice and image: not a thank-you note reworded,
  but a light left on for a reader who has spent the day reading everyone
  else's words.
